Computer Vision Consultant

Not sure if your vision idea is feasible? Hassan provides hands-on consulting — audits, architecture, and production guidance for computer vision projects.

What Does a Computer Vision Consultant Do?

A consultant turns uncertainty into a plan. Vision projects fail for predictable reasons — unclear feasibility, wrong model or hardware choice, latency nobody budgeted for, data that doesn't match the task. Hassan's consulting exists to catch those problems before they cost you months.

The work is hands-on, not slideware: he reviews your data, prototypes the riskiest part of your pipeline, and gives you an honest verdict on feasibility, accuracy, latency and cost. You leave with either a working direction or a clear reason to stop.

Feasibility & Scoping

Every engagement starts with the same question: is this achievable, and at what price? Hassan assesses your inputs — camera placement, lighting, scene density, motion speed, hardware budget — and maps them to what today's models can actually deliver.

This phase is deliberately concrete. If the answer is "feasible with caveats", you get the caveats and a plan to de-risk them. If it's "not with current data", you learn that early, with options. See what is computer vision for baseline expectations, and how to hire a vision engineer for the hiring side of the equation.

Architecture & Tech Selection

Once feasibility is clear, the next trap is architecture. Hassan reviews or designs the stack end to end: model choice (detection, tracking, pose), inference runtime (ONNX Runtime, TensorRT), hardware (edge box vs. cloud), and the serving layer (Edge AI, FastAPI, streaming).

He has opinions backed by shipped systems — including sub-30ms real-time pipelines at ID Sports Ventures. Whether it's real-time video analytics or edge AI inference, the review is grounded in what actually runs in production.

Getting Models to Production

The biggest gap in most vision projects is the last mile: a model that works in a notebook but falls apart under real load. Hassan audits and fixes the production path — quantization and TensorRT compilation, Docker packaging, AWS deployment, and the testing discipline that keeps pipelines honest (85% test coverage in his own work).

  • Pipeline audits — find the bottlenecks killing latency, accuracy or reliability.
  • Model optimization — quantization and runtime tuning for target hardware.
  • Deployment guidance — containerized serving on edge and cloud, tested and repeatable.
  • Honest reports — plain-language findings your leadership and engineers can act on.

Engagement Models

Hassan keeps consulting flexible, matching the depth of involvement to where your project is stuck:

Fractional consulting — a recurring slice of senior vision engineering for teams that need ongoing architectural direction. Fixed-scope engagements — a defined deliverable with a defined price: a feasibility study, an architecture review, a production audit. Team training — hands-on sessions that get your engineers productive with PyTorch, YOLO, ByteTrack and deployment tooling.
